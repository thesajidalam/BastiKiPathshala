About Us

Transforming Lives Through Education Since 2015

Basti Ki Pathshala Foundation is a non-profit organisation dedicated to providing quality education to underprivileged children in urban slums and rural areas. We believe every child — regardless of socio-economic background — deserves access to learning, opportunity and dignity.

What We Do

Our Programs

A complete support system that helps children learn, grow and thrive.

Quality Education

Remedial classes, homework support and foundational literacy in a safe learning space.

Life Skills

Confidence, communication, hygiene and values that prepare children for life beyond school.

Nutrition Support

Healthy meals and nutrition awareness so children can learn with energy and focus.

Digital Literacy

Basic computer and internet skills to open doors in a digital-first world.
